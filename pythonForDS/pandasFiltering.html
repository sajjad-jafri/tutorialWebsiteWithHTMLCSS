🧹 Filtering

Filtering allows you to:

  • Extract rows that match a condition (e.g., age > 30)

  • Combine multiple conditions (AND / OR logic)

  • Clean data by removing unwanted or invalid entries


Let's take a sample dataframe to proceed further

	Name    Age    City
Emp 1   Alice   25     New York
Emp 2   Bob     30     Paris
Emp 3   Charlie 45     London
Emp 4   Greg    42     Washigton
Emp 5   Harry   22     London


Filter df with one or multiple condition

# Filter by one condition
old_emp = df[df["Age"]>40]
print(old_emp)

# Filter by multiple condition
old_lon_emp = df[(df["Age"]>40) |
                 (df["City"]=="London")]
print(old_lon_emp)

Output

# old_emp
      Name  Age        City
2  Charlie   45      London
3     Greg   42  Washington

# old_lon_emp
      Name  Age        City
2  Charlie   45      London
3     Greg   42  Washington
4    Harry   22      London

Note: | --> OR, & --> AND



Filter by startswith

New_York_emp = df[df["City"].str.startswith("N")]
print(New_York_emp)

Output

    Name  Age      City
0  Alice   25  New York
